After the umpteenth time of her being kidnapped (of which the specific number is far too high to keep count) by some spiky-mutated-turtle-dragon-what-the-fuck-is-that-thing-just-kill-it, my client has been greatly irritated—a completely normal and warranted response—by her hopeless ineptitude in securing the castle. Though these recurrent incidents have only begun after their marriage, similar troubles had been plaguing their relationship from day one. As I recall, during our meeting to finalize the papers, he had explained that it all began during their college days, before their first game was conceived.

***
In some remote corner of some generic rave, the mushroom takes a hit from his joint before passing it onto his purportedly Italian friend. Of course, at the present moment, he’s utterly oblivious as to how his smoking habits will shape his iconic voice, but that’s not what this story is about. His friend had been repeatedly trying to convince him of his heritage, though he’s is fully aware that _Mamma Mia_ directly translates to _Oh Mommy_, which would make his friend quite the wuss, in addition to being a suspect of falsifying his own heritage. In any case, after Toadette had given him shit for calling the Goombas, “Gooms”—which apparently is not okay anymore—he started spending more time outside of the house, chain smoking with his friend afflicted with the Oedipus complex.

The Italian dwarf, on the other hand, had been having some doubts about the virtues of his own endeavors. Peach only recently criticized him for perpetuating colonialist agendas, running amok in various levels, slaughtering the objectively inferior races—or so his prejudiced convictions had appealed. In what seemed to him a rapidly evolving society, he learned to find some comfort in his conservative fungal friend.

“Look, bru—it’s the only way to rake in the cash.” He speaks in somber tones, which isn’t reflective of his mood, insofar as he’s somewhat stoned at this point. “How much property tax does she think we pay for the goddamn castle...”

Bass heavy music pervades the stale air. The mushroom takes a sharp hit, pushing the smoke through his nostrils. He coughs up some phlegm. Spits. Snifles. He could never fully get rid of the wad of mucus lodged at the back of his throat. He didn’t particularly care for his friend’s relationship, and never truly understood why she settled for a guy like him, especially when he was less than a third of her height—without the heels. In fact, just the day before, he had overheard the two quarreling on campus. They were among many naked peers littering the lawn; the UV index was five.

The performative Italian had proposed increasing surveillance surrounding the warp tubes as a form of counter-terrorism, but as he expected, she framed it as a form of mass monitoring. He found this anti-authoritarian argument odd, especially coming from her, given that she had miraculously “won” all the previous elections. He suspected her of implementing strict proof-of-citizenship requirements to suppress votes from the Toads in poverty. The midget knew of her devious stratagems. He wasn’t backing down.

As the argument evolved into an increasingly heated one, the absurdity of the punches they were trading kept apace, until at last, she brought up the topic that he had dreaded most.

“I don’t understand why you can’t keep yourself mushroom-ed!!” And here comes the devastating blow: “You know I can’t feel anything!!”

***
According to my client, this marked the beginning of the end. Had he been more mushroom-ed, perhaps none of this would have played out, but there’s no use in ruminating. As he signed the papers, sniffling, shaking with tears, he took his cap off to reveal a barren landscape. And it was at that moment that I had lost all hope in any prospects of marriage.
